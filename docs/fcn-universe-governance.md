# FCN Universe Governance

The daily recommendation pool is deliberately smaller than the searchable US
equity universe. Search coverage is not a reason to enlarge the scheduled pool.

## Review cadence

- Monthly health review: option liquidity, quote quality, search demand,
  persistent non-GO status, and concentration by sector/theme.
- Quarterly IC review after earnings season: tier, active status, holdability,
  and documented status reason.
- Semiannual rebalance: replace duplicated or structurally weak names before
  increasing the pool beyond 50-65 active underlyings.
- Event-driven review: earnings warning, regulatory restriction, delisting
  concern, material business-model deterioration, or option-liquidity failure.

## Admission and retention

An active name should be recognizable or thematically relevant to PB clients,
emotionally holdable after assignment, and supported by a usable listed-option
chain. High implied volatility alone is not an admission criterion.

New listings remain search-only until at least 120 trading bars are available;
the daily pool normally requires two public reporting cycles and enough history
to evaluate a representative FCN path window.

`under_review` names remain searchable for RM analysis but are excluded from the
daily screener. Returning to `active` requires an IC review record and observable
trend/fundamental re-confirmation. Nationality or ADR status alone must not lower
the model grade.

## China ADR presentation policy

- `adr_risk` is a governance marker, not an automatic score penalty.
- Weak-trend China ADRs receive a tactical daily-ranking penalty.
- The daily showcase may contain at most one China ADR exposure.
- Sector-level regime data can be added later, but must be structured and
  downgrade-only rather than a manually tuned nationality penalty.
